The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy is a very fun and weird book to read. It starts off with Arthur, a somewhat boring character who's day sucks. With the impending doom of the earth ending (which sci-fi book doesn't go for on a planet exploding), and with the help of his friend Ford, he hitches a ride on a spaceship to start a whole adventure through space and time.

The book has a weird and sometimes disturbing way of writing, where the main story is intertwined with excerpts from the Hitchhiker's Guide and other perspectives, which sometimes go on for a long time. The story however, is very fun. The galaxy as portrayed by Douglas Adams is one of the weirdest places imaginable and that's what makes it fun.

The galaxy you're expecting is totally different from Adam's perspective on the galaxy. Here, capitalism and bureaucracy rule the galaxy and stupid, meaningless inventions and technology are mainstream. Take the Nutrimatic Drinks Dispenser, a machine that works out what you want to drink but isn't capable of making a cup of tea. The story takes us through different places, times, wars and all sorts of crazy space stuff. The most likeable thing in this book is truly the weirdness of every single thing you encounter.

I love the crazy take on the galaxy in this book, but the writing sometimes put me off a bit. I've read the Ultimate Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, a bundle with all the different books, which is a long read. The story is great and fun to follow along and you never expect what'll happen next.
