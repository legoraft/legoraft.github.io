Because I publish different things to this site, I have different RSS feeds for each. You can select your preferred one from the list:

[Everything](/rss.xml) - All the content I publish on my site.

[Posts](/posts/rss.xml) - Blog posts about whatever interests me at that time.

[Books](/books/rss.xml) - Book reviews on books I've read recently.

RSS is one of the best ways to follow the content you're looking for and you should have the choice on what to follow (including on a single site). All the content should be available through RSS, but please let me know if you're encountering any issues!

My reader of choice is [FeedFlow](https://www.feedflow.dev/), an open source reader. I combine this with [FreshRSS](https://freshrss.org/) to sync my feeds to everywhere.
