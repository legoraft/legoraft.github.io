At the beginning of this summer vacation I treated myself on a new lens for my camera. The lens I got was a [Canon EF 50mm f/1.8 STM](https://global.canon/en/c-museum/product/ef451.html) which has a low aperture and a fixed focal length. I wanted to restrain myself and focus more on composition, while also giving me more options with the bokeh effect a low-aperture lens comes with. This lens was truly one of the best purchases I've ever made.

## My setup

![A Canon EOS 1300D with a 50mm f/1.8 lens.](./SNY_00005.avif)

During my vacation I was still using the old camera I'd got for about €300 in 2022. This is a Canon 1300D (or Rebel T6 in some regions) and it has treated me, well, _fine_. It has horrible low light performance, but is perfectly functional and for the limited budget I was on, it's a great camera. I've used it with the default 18-55mm lens for a long time and got a 70-300mm lens later on. I just really couldn't fix the not-so-great low light performance. This was where the 50mm comes in.

The 50mm is often called the "nifty-fifty", because it is so versatile in so many situations. With my 1.6x cropped APS-C sensor, it was a little too tight for my taste, but it worked great and I really understand the name of the lens. Adding this to my camera also had two extra reasons: I wanted a more compact carry for everyday shooting, so I'd make more pictures.

And oh boy, did I make pictures. This vacation I went to Normandy in France, Prague, Hungary, Bosnia, Croatia and Ljubljana, which got me a lot of pictures. There were moments I needed to switch to my phone, because the lens was too tight, but I got a lot of great shots. I'm just dropping a few of my personal favorites here to share.

## Further

This lens was truly an amazing purchase. I've rediscovered the love I have for photography and I'm purchasing a better camera, mostly for lower-light pictures and using it more. I love the prime lens, which has enough constraints to focus on the art, not just zooming in and out and taking 20 pictures of the same thing, all at a different focal length. I truly recommend everyone to try a prime lens at some point or another.
